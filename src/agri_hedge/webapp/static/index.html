How it works

PRF Companion: enter where your land is and the PRF intervals you insure. We test, month by month, how well Kalshi rain contracts at the nearest settlement station track rain on your PRF grid in the months PRF leaves open, recommend the months that track well, and build one order for them. Custom cover: pick any months and build a stand-alone hedge.

Each answer takes a few seconds: every hedge is refit 75 times to test it on years it has not seen.
