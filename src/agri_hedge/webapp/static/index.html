3. Price and fit

How it works

PRF Companion: enter where your land is and the PRF intervals you insure. We test, month by month, how well Kalshi rain contracts at the nearest settlement station track rain on your PRF grid in the months PRF leaves open, recommend the months that track well, and build one order for them. Custom cover: pick any months and build a stand-alone hedge. Design a contract: choose a payout shape (linear like PRF, convex, step-up tiers, your farm's loss curve, or the data-optimal shape) for one month and get the term sheet, the exact replicating order and your basis risk. Ask the advisor: describe your farm in a sentence and let the advisor pick and design the contract.

Companion and custom hedge fit is tested on years left out of training. Contract design shows its station payout, order fill and farm basis risk separately.
