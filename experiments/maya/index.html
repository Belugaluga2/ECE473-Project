Why that coefficient?

The coefficient sizes the contract to a chosen price budget. It is not a measured biological effect of weather on corn.

Once the payout shape has one coefficient and its expected-payout budget is fixed, this arithmetic determines the coefficient. With several features, constrained regression chooses how to divide the budget.

We chose the payout family using earlier temporal validation. Later data did not choose the winner. Regularization discourages unstable coefficients; it does not guarantee generalization.

What the evidence actually supports

The special interaction has not convincingly beaten simpler protection. Removing 2012 makes the selected policy worse than no hedge on the premium-inclusive squared-error measure. These outcomes have already been inspected; further changes need separate confirmation.

Why exact static replication has a mathematical gap

Any separate payment f(D) + g(H) has zero rectangular cross-difference. Our min(D,H) rule does not: its four endpoint payments are 0, 0, 0 and the maximum payment.

That is a bound over the whole severity domain, not a prediction of typical realized error. Daily-high contracts also omit Tmin, which our duration-based heat index needs. A smart contract can execute a formula; it cannot remove these input, market or farm-loss mismatches.
